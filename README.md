# Love Story Atlas

Turn My WhatsApp Chat Into a Premium Relationship Magazine

I have attached a complete WhatsApp chat export between two people. The export may contain:

Daily conversations

Good morning / good night messages

Photos

Videos

Voice note references

Emojis

Stickers

Shared media

Attachments

Date & time stamps

Your task is NOT to summarize the chat.

Instead, create a beautifully designed 6–8 page premium PDF magazine that tells the story of this relationship through data, memories, and storytelling.

The final output should feel like a mix of:

Spotify Wrapped

A luxury coffee-table magazine

Relationship analytics

Prioritize beautiful design and storytelling over simply listing statistics.
